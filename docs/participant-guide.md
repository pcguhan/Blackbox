# Participant guide

This guide is for players. The same information is shown in the app at `/guide`, which is linked from the account menu and the navigation bar.

## Signing in

The organisers choose which sign-in methods are available. The login page shows only the methods that are switched on.

- **Google**: choose **Continue with Google** and sign in with your `@vitstudent.ac.in` account. Your account is created on the first sign-in. Your name and roll number come from your Google profile; your username defaults to your roll number.
- **Local account**: register with a username, email and password, then confirm your email address from the link the platform sends you. Use **Forgot password** to reset it by email.

If the organisers have made the event **Google-only**, local registration, local sign-in and password reset are unavailable to players, and a signed-in local session ends on its next page load.

Already have a verified local account with your student email? Signing in with Google for that email links Google to your existing account; your team and points stay. An unverified local account cannot be linked; verify it by email first.

If the event uses an **access list**, only listed email addresses can sign in. You will see "Event going on right now, you don't have access." if yours is not listed.

## Nickname and profile picture

Open **Profile settings** from the account menu to set an optional nickname and upload a profile picture (PNG, JPEG or WebP; the image is re-encoded and metadata is removed). Your nickname appears next to your username in team member lists. It is a display label only.

## Teams

The leaderboard ranks **teams**, not individual players.

- **Create a team** from **Teams → Create team**. You become the captain and receive an invite code.
- **Join a team** with the invite code from a teammate (**Teams → Join team**). Teams may have a password and a size limit set by the organisers.
- Some challenges, or the whole event, may require you to be in a team before you can open them.

Every member's solves count for the team. A challenge is solved once per team: when one member solves it, it is marked solved for everyone, and a second correct submission by a teammate does not award points again.

## Challenges and flags

- Browse challenges by category, search by name, and filter by solved state on the **Challenges** page.
- Flags use the format **`CYS{...}`** unless a challenge says otherwise. Organisers can change the default prefix or use a different format for a single challenge, so follow the instructions on the challenge page.
- Some challenges are released in acts, or unlock after you solve another challenge or submit a particular flag. Locked challenges are not shown.

## Hints

Hints can cost points. Unlocking a hint deducts its cost from your team's score, and the hint is then visible to your whole team. Some hints become available only after an earlier hint.

## Container challenges

Some challenges start a private instance for you:

1. Choose **Start** on the challenge page. The connection details (URL or `nc` command) appear when it is ready.
2. Each instance has its own flag. A flag from someone else's instance will not be accepted for you.
3. **Restart** resets your instance, subject to a short cooldown. Instances stop automatically when they expire and, if the organisers enable it, when your team solves the challenge.

The number of instances that can run at once is limited, so stop yours when you are done.

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard updates live. It shows each team's rank, score, solve count and first bloods (being the first team to solve a challenge, which may earn bonus points). Ties are broken by whichever team reached the score first. Organisers can freeze the leaderboard near the end of the event; while it is frozen, you see the standings from the moment of the freeze.

## Event timing

Challenges are available only while the event is running. Before the start, a countdown is shown; while the event is paused or after it ends, challenges and submissions are closed.
